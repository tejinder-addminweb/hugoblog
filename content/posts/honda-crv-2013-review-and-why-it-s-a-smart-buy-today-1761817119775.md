## Understanding the Value of the 2013 Honda CR-V

When looking at a used car, especially one that’s over a decade old, it’s essential to consider its condition, mileage, and market value. The 2013 Honda CR-V is known for its durability and low maintenance costs, making it a popular choice for families and commuters alike. 

The model you mentioned, the **EX Sport Utility 4D**, is particularly appealing. With a 2.4L 4-cylinder engine, it strikes a good balance between power and fuel efficiency. Given that it has 141,792 miles, it’s not uncommon for a vehicle of this age, and many owners report that their CR-Vs last well beyond 200,000 miles with regular maintenance.

### What’s the Market Saying?

Before diving into whether $7.5k is a fair price, let’s look at the broader market. According to various online resources, the average price for a 2013 Honda CR-V typically ranges from $12,000 to $15,000, depending on the condition and location. ## Assessing the Deal

So, is this a solid deal or something to be wary of? Here are a few things to consider:

1. **Vehicle History Report**: Always check for any accidents, service records, or title issues. Resources like Carfax can provide valuable insights.
  
2. **Pre-Purchase Inspection**: Before making any purchase, especially for a used car, it's wise to have a trusted mechanic inspect it. This could save you from unexpected repairs.

3. **Market Comparison**: Research similar listings in your area to see if the price aligns. Websites like Kelley Blue Book and Edmunds can help you understand if the deal stands out.

- **Fuel Efficiency**: The 2013 CR-V offers good gas mileage, averaging around 23 mpg in the city and 31 mpg on the highway.

- **Safety Ratings**: Honda has a solid reputation for safety, and the 2013 CR-V is no exception. It received good ratings in crash tests, which is always a crucial factor when considering a purchase.
